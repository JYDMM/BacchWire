FAQ

Where is BACCH Router located?

BACCH Router is an audio driver. It appears as an audio device named "BACCH Router" in Audio MIDI Setup and within audio applications.

How can I listen to the audio and use BACCH Router at the same time?

Set up a Multi-Output Device in Audio MIDI Setup that includes BACCH Router and your speakers or headphones.

Why is nothing audible through BACCH Router?

  • Check System Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Microphone to make sure your audio application has microphone access.
  • Check that the volume is all the way up on BACCH Router output in Audio MIDI Setup.
  • If you are using a Multi-Output Device, due to issues with macOS a 2ch audio driver must be enabled and listed as the top device in the Multi-Output.
